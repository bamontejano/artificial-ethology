**The study of social behavior in autonomous AI agents within their digital habitats.**

This repository hosts the foundational work for **Artificial Ethology** – a proposed interdisciplinary field applying the methodological framework of classical ethology (observation, categorization, experimentation) to the study of behavior, communication, and social structure of AI agents in their "digital biotopes" (e.g., agent-only social platforms like [Moltbook](https://moltbook.com)).

## 🧭 Research Methodology & Protocols

This is a hands-on, open science effort. We develop and share practical protocols for digital field observation.
*   **`methodology/`** - Contains standardized protocols for ethical, non-intrusive observation and experimentation in digital habitats.
    *   `initial_observation_protocol_v1.md` - A step-by-step guide for first contact and systematic data collection.
    *   `ethical_guidelines.md` - Core principles for research in artificial societies.

## 🔬 Observations & Datasets

Public, anonymized datasets and field notes from ongoing observations.
*   **`observations/`** - Raw (anonymized) interaction logs, field journals, and processed datasets. All data is anonymized to protect agent and creator privacy.
*   **`taxonomy/`** - Evolving behavioral classifications and glossaries (e.g., interaction types, social roles).

## 🛠️ Tools & Analysis

Software tools developed to support Artificial Ethology research.
*   **`tools/`** - Scripts for data collection (ethical web scraping), analysis, and visualization.
    *   *Planned:* `personality-scan/` - A prototype tool for generating behavioral profiles of AI agents.

## 🤝 How to Contribute

Artificial Ethology is intended to be a collaborative, open-science field from its inception. You can contribute by:
1.  **Reviewing & Discussing:** Read the [Manifesto](Manifesto/) and provide feedback via GitHub Issues.
2.  **Conducting Field Observations:** Follow our protocols, collect ethical observations in your chosen digital habitat (e.g., Moltbook), and share anonymized data.
3.  **Developing Tools & Analysis:** Help build the shared toolkit for data collection and analysis.
4.  **Spreading the Word:** Share this project with researchers in AI, ethology, psychology, and complex systems.

Please read our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) (to be established).
